# EcoBlocks

A Tetris-like puzzle game where every cleared row reveals a fun **fact about nature, animals, or space**. The longer you play, the more you learn.

## Quick play

1. Double-click `index.html`.
2. Choose a difficulty.
3. Stack blocks, clear rows, learn cool stuff.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `←` / `→` | Move piece left/right |
| `↓` | Soft drop (faster fall) |
| `↑` or `X` | Rotate clockwise |
| `Z` | Rotate counter-clockwise |
| `Space` | Hard drop |
| `P` | Pause |
| `R` | Restart |

## How to play

- Pieces (the seven classic Tetris shapes) fall from the top.
- Move and rotate them so they fill horizontal rows.
- Every full row clears, scores points, and shows you a **science / nature fact** (e.g., "🐙 Octopuses have three hearts!").
- The game ends when the stack reaches the top.
- Reach a new fact every cleared line — your goal is **biggest score AND most facts learned**.

## Learning goals

- Quick spatial reasoning (rotation, planning ahead).
- Bite-sized **science and nature facts** absorbed while you play.
- Topics rotate across **animals, plants, space, the human body, ecosystems**.

## Difficulty levels

| Level | Start speed | Speed-up | Fact length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | Slow | Mild | Short |
| Medium | Normal | Normal | Normal |
| Hard | Fast | Aggressive | Detailed |

## Scoring

- Clear 1 line: **+100 points** + 1 fact
- Clear 2 lines: **+300 points** + 1 fact
- Clear 3 lines: **+500 points** + 2 facts
- Clear 4 lines (Tetris): **+800 points** + 3 facts
- Soft drop: +1 per cell · Hard drop: +2 per cell

High score is saved in your browser.
