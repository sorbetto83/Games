# Math Quest

A 2D space-platformer where the only way past the energy gates is to **answer arithmetic problems** correctly. Every gate you clear earns coins; every star you grab boosts your score.

## Quick play

1. Double-click `index.html`.
2. Pick a difficulty.
3. Run, jump, answer math!

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `←` / `→` | Move left / right |
| `Space` or `↑` | Jump |
| `0`–`9` | Type answer at a math gate |
| `Backspace` | Erase a digit |
| `Enter` | Submit answer |
| `P` | Pause |
| `R` | Restart current level |

## How to play

- Your astronaut starts on the left side of a level.
- Run to the right, jumping on platforms and avoiding pits.
- When you reach a glowing **math gate**, the game pauses and shows you a problem (e.g., `7 + 8 = ?`).
- Type the answer with the number keys, then press `Enter`.
- ✅ Correct → the gate opens, you earn coins, and you keep running.
- ❌ Wrong → you lose a heart. Three wrong answers and you have to restart.
- Reach the rocket at the end of the level to win!

## Learning goals

- Mental arithmetic under (gentle) time pressure.
- Number fluency: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division.
- Difficulty levels scale the math, not the reflexes — kids who struggle with platformers can still progress.

## Difficulty levels

| Level | Math operations | Number range | Time pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | + and − | 0–10 | None |
| Medium | + − × | 0–20 | Mild |
| Hard | + − × ÷ | 0–50 | Stronger |

## Scoring

- ✅ Correct answer: **+100 points**
- ⭐ Star collected: **+25 points**
- 🪙 Coin collected: **+10 points**
- 🚀 Level completed: **+500 points**
- ❌ Wrong answer: **−25 points** (and lose a heart)

High score is saved in your browser.
